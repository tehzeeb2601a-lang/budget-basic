SMART SPENDING

Need or Want?

One of the most important budgeting skills is understanding the difference between things you really need and things you simply want.

✅

Needs

Things that are important for daily life and education.

  • Food
  • Transport
  • Books
✨

Wants

Things that are enjoyable but may not be necessary.

  • Gaming
  • Extra Shopping
  • Entertainment

BUDGETING BASICS

Understand Your Money

Budget banane ke liye sabse pehle apni income, expenses, needs, wants aur savings ko samajhna zaroori hai.

💰

Income

Income wo paisa hai jo aapko allowance, scholarship, part-time work ya kisi aur source se milta hai.

Example: Rs. 20,000
📌

Fixed Expenses

Aise expenses jo har month mostly same rehte hain aur jinhein plan karna easy hota hai.

Example: Transport
🛍️

Variable Expenses

Aise expenses jo har month change ho sakte hain, jaise food, shopping aur entertainment.

Example: Shopping
🎯

Savings

Income ka wo hissa jo future goals aur unexpected needs ke liye save kiya jata hai.

Example: Rs. 4,000

50-30-20 Budget Rule

A simple way to divide your monthly income

50%

Needs

Use 50% of your income for important expenses like food, transport and education.

30%

Wants

Use 30% for things you enjoy, such as shopping, entertainment and hobbies.

20%

Savings

Keep 20% of your income for savings and future goals.
